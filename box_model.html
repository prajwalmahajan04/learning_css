Animals are living organisms that can be found in almost every part of the world. They live in forests, oceans, deserts, grasslands, and even near human homes. Animals are divided into different groups, such as mammals, birds, reptiles, amphibians, fish, and insects. Some animals, like cows, dogs, and cats, live with humans, while others, such as lions, tigers, and elephants, live mainly in the wild. Animals are important for maintaining the balance of nature because they are part of food chains and ecosystems. They also provide humans with food, clothing materials, companionship, and other useful resources. We should protect animals and their natural habitats and treat them with kindness and care.
